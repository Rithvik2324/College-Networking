Rules + Governance

The platform is built to reward execution, not popularity.

This page explains how communities move through their lifecycle and how the network stays clear, useful, and low-noise.

01. Group Lifecycle

Every group has a clear path so it stays useful.

Create Start a group around one goal and one timeline.
Execute Assign tasks, define checkpoints, and keep shipping.
Complete Close the sprint with outputs, notes, and outcomes.
Archive Inactive teams are closed before they become dead weight.

02. Network Rules

The platform is built to protect collaboration quality.

  • College email verification keeps the network campus-only.
  • No follower counts, likes, or vanity ranking systems.
  • No promotions or spam inside execution spaces.
  • Inactive groups are automatically archived.
Execution-first norms Inactivity-based removal Psychological safety Low-noise environment

03. Student Benefits

Students get speed, clarity, and better collaboration quality.

  • Less time searching for teammates and more time building.
  • Higher chance of finishing hackathons and campus projects.
  • Cross-year collaboration without relying on personal circles.
  • A cleaner way to discover serious collaborators.

04. Platform Safeguards

The system actively protects collaboration quality as usage grows.

  • Gradual rollout keeps moderation manageable.
  • Automation handles inactive groups before clutter grows.
  • Intent switching helps students stay relevant over time.
  • Group-size limits prevent low-accountability chaos.
